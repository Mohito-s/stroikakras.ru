## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the precision, permanence, and restrained grandeur of bespoke stone masonry and architectural bathroom construction. Built for high-net-worth homeowners, architects, and luxury interior designers, the aesthetic delivers an uncompromising standard of craft: clean mitered edges, rectified joint lines, and seamless plane transitions translated into digital ergonomics.

The visual language draws from **Architectural Minimalism** married to **Tactile Luxury**. Instead of heavy skeuomorphic effects, it leverages deep graphite slabs, obsidian planes, ultra-fine calibrated border lines (echoing 1mm epoxy grout joints), and glowing accents of brushed artisan brass. Surfaces feel heavy, dense, and structural, invoking the permanence of honed marble, sintered stone, and hand-rubbed metallic fixtures. Interfaces must project exacting competence: balanced proportions, ample breathing room, and razor-sharp typographic discipline.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic monolithic foundation, relying on mineral tones rather than pure artificial blacks.

- **Foundational Slabs**: The canvas root is deep obsidian slate (`#121316`), overlaid with structured surface tiers in graphite (`#1A1C21`), honed basalt (`#23262D`), and wet mortar (`#2E323B`). These gradations provide natural plane separation without visual noise.
- **Architectural Accents**: Brushed brass (`#C5A059`) serves as the primary focal tone for high-intent interactive drivers, with luminous polished gold (`#D4AF37`) reserved for micro-interactions, precision states, and focal highlights.
- **Mineral Neutrals**: Text and vector iconography rely on travertine white (`#F4F4F6`), limestone mist (`#C8C9CE`), and muted slate veining (`#8C8D91`) for secondary meta-information.
- **Joinery Borders**: All container dividers and frame borders use a controlled metallic or stone seam (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` on interactive states; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` on static structural grids).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is grounded in high-contrast editorial elegance juxtaposed with hyper-rational functional information:

- **Display & Section Lead Titles**: **Bodoni Moda** provides sharp, high-fashion transitional contrast, mirroring the fine, chiseled precision of waterjet-cut sintered stone and bookmatched Italian marble slabs. It is always set with tight structural tracking and generous line heights.
- **Operational & Informational Interface**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** anchors interactive UI elements, specs, cost estimates, dimensions, and long-form consultation copy. Its clean, geometric geometry prevents fatigue while retaining architectural refinement.
- **Specification Badges & Micro-Labels**: Set using `label-caps` in uppercase with wide tracking (`0.15em`), capturing the technical blueprint markings of custom construction schedules, tile caliber codes, and wet-area certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts conform to a disciplined modular tile grid, treating empty space as deliberate architectural negative volume.

- **Grid Strategy**: An 8-point structural system driving a responsive 4-column layout on mobile (<768px), 8-column layout on tablet (768px–1024px), and 12-column layout on desktop (>1024px). Maximum container width is pinned at 1440px to preserve photographic fidelity of large-format slabs.
- **Grid Gaps as Grout Lines**: Components and interactive cards sit in tight, calculated proximity (using `space-sm` and `space-md`), reminiscent of 1/16-inch or 1mm minimal rectified installations.
- **Negative Space Breathing Room**: Outer frame margins (`space-xl` through `space-xxl`) are preserved rigorously to convey exclusivity and luxury. Elements never crowd viewport limits. Mobile screens employ edge-to-edge full bleed imagery broken by anchored, high-contrast structural text cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material stratification rather than blurry, disembodied drop shadows.

- **Planar Stacking**: Surfaces climb through tonal darkness. The ground plane is obsidian (`#121316`). Level 1 modules (cards, architectural sidebars) sit in graphite (`#1A1C21`). Floating interactive sheets and dropdown menus emerge in basalt (`#23262D`).
- **Precision Seams (Mitered Ghost Lines)**: Instead of thick dropshadows, layers delineate themselves via ultra-crisp 1px borders. These borders utilize subtle mineral luminosity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) on inactive surfaces and warm brushed brass wireframes (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`) on focused or elevated states.
- **Warm Specular Glow**: When an element demands emphasis (such as a pinned estimation bar or modal lightbox), depth is reinforced with a localized brass ambient halo: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is strictly calibrated at **Level 0 (Sharp)**, honoring the pure, pristine geometry of rectified porcelain slabs, exact 45-degree miter joints, and cut architectural glass:

- All cards, containers, input boxes, badges, and buttons utilize crisp right angles (`0px` border radius).
- Rounded or pill-shaped geometries are strictly prohibited, as soft curves dilute the tactile perception of diamond-blade masonry, waterjet accuracy, and plumb alignment.
- Visual rhythm is created through miter-inspired accents: subtle 45-degree chamfered corners may be applied on primary showcase badges and interactive hero buttons via CSS clip-paths.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brushed Brass)**: Background of `#C5A059`, text in deep `#121316`, `label-md` font weight, zero border-radius. On hover, background transitions to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`). Inner padding: `14px 28px`.
- **Secondary Action (Mitered Outline)**: Transparent background, 1px solid border of `#C5A059`, text in `#F4F4F6`. Hover triggers a surface tint of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.1)`.
- **Ghost Action**: Monolithic graphite `#1A1C21` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border; text in `#C8C9CE`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Background set to `#1A1C21` with a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- Cards maintain zero border-radius. Image containers feature edge-to-edge aspect ratios (4:5 or 16:9) highlighting bookmatched veining, seamless niche transitions, and drain integration.
- Hover states transition the border seam to `#C5A059` and slightly elevate image scale (1.02x) within an overflow-hidden frame.

### Chips & Technical Badges
- Compact rectangular indicators for material grades (e.g., "CALACATTA VIOLA", "SLAB SIZE: 160x320cm", "EPOXY GROUTED").
- Styled with obsidian backgrounds, `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`, typography set to `label-caps` in `#C5A059`, and padding of `4px 10px`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Dark slate surface `#16181D` paired with an inset bottom hairline or complete rectangular border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Labels float in `label-sm` using `#8C8D91`. Upon focus, borders transition crisply to `#C5A059` without offset rings. Placeholders appear in dimmed limestone (`#565860`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom 16x16px squares (zero radius). Unselected states feature an empty field with a 1px border in `#565860`.
- Selected states fill solid with `#C5A059`, displaying a sharp, contrasting black checkmark or an inner concentric square for radio toggles.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Before/After Joint Comparison Slider**: Interactive dual-plane image viewport anchored by a brass hairline divider and a 28px square brass scrubber handle, highlighting microscopic epoxy grout lines and miter cuts.
- **Specification Matrix / Cost Ledger**: High-density data grid featuring alternating rows of `#1A1C21` and `#121316`, separated by 1px borders resembling technical architectural blueprints, with currency/measurements rendered in tabular monospace figures.
