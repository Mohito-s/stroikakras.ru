## Brand & Style

This design system embodies Scandinavian pragmatism, warmth, and meticulous artisanal precision. It speaks to homeowners seeking a trustworthy, seasoned craftsman for bathroom renovations and precision tile work—someone whose reputation relies on laser-straight joints, honest pricing, and immaculate cleanliness after the work is done.

The visual mood steers entirely clear of sterile corporate gloss and opulent "luxury" pretense. Instead, it projects honest mastercraft through calm, natural tones, ample breathable space, architectural alignment, and tactile warmth. The interface reflects the artisan’s core work ethic: orderliness, transparency, reliability, and human approachability.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from Nordic wet rooms, natural stone, sanded grout, and workwear fabrics:

- **Primary (`#1E4756` - Deep Fjord Blue):** A stable, composed workwear blue used for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, and structural keypoints. It conveys dependability without cold corporate detachment.
- **Secondary (`#5B6C5D` - Muted Olive Grout):** An earthy, organic sage-olive tone used for secondary actions, reassurance tags, guarantee indicators, and process completion badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D8CAB8` - Sanded Travertine):** Warm stone beige used for soft surface cards, price tag backgrounds, and subtle highlight blocks.
- **Neutral (`#272A2B` - Graphite Carbon):** A softened black providing maximum legibility for typography without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm white (`#FBFBF9`) paired with light architectural gray (`#F2F1ED`) for nested containers to replicate clean matte porcelain and pristine grout lines.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric foundation offers clean clarity akin to laser-leveled tiling, while subtle rounded apertures prevent it from feeling clinical or intimidating.

- **Headlines:** Set with confident semi-bold and bold weights to ground the page structure, evoking precision and mastery.
- **Body:** Kept open with comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate effortless reading of scopes of work, estimates, and project diaries.
- **Labels and Metrics:** Compact, legible, and slightly emphasized for specs (e.g., tile dimensions, project duration, guarantee terms).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the craftsmanship of tiling: disciplined alignment, consistent gaps, and clean negative space.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width container of 1200px centered on a 12-column layout. Gutter is `1.5rem` (24px) with section margins starting at `2.5rem` (40px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with compact `1rem` (16px) gutters and screen margins to maximize usable area on handheld devices.

Spacers adhere strictly to an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), creating an unmistakable sense of order, neatness, and architectural rhythm throughout the page.

## Elevation & Depth

To express the honesty and physical reality of construction materials, this system rejects artificial, heavy dropshadows and glossy blurs.

- **Tonal Layering:** Visual depth is achieved via tactile surface stacking. The page background sits on `#FBFBF9`, surface cards rest on `#FFFFFF`, and nested calculation or specification panels use `#F2F1ED` or `#F7F4EE`.
- **Low-Contrast Tile Outlines:** Cards and structured blocks use ultra-subtle 1px borders (`#E5E3DD`) reminiscent of uniform 1.5mm tile joints.
- **Soft Ambient Support:** When interactive elements float or lift (e.g., photo galleries, sticky quote calculation bars, modals), they use a soft, grounded shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(39, 42, 43, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a balanced `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px standard corner radius). 

This represents the softly chamfered edge of a fine ceramic or porcelain tile—crisp enough to look precise and geometric, yet softened enough to feel friendly, safe, and modern. Secondary interactive chips and badges utilize slightly softer pill-like contours (`rounded-xl` or full pills) to stand apart from the architectural rectangular grids of portfolios and pricing cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E4756` (Deep Fjord), label in white, 8px border radius, vertical padding `0.75rem`, horizontal padding `1.5rem`. Hover state slightly darkens to `#163541` with smooth transition.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#F2F1ED`, 1px border `#D8CAB8`, text `#1E4756`. Used for exploratory actions like "View Tile Catalog" or "Read Reviews".
- **Direct Contact / WhatsApp Accent:** Background `#5B6C5D` with white text for pragmatic, reassuring human contact.

### Cards & Work Showcases
- **Before/After Portfolio Cards:** Clean white container `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E5E3DD` border. Interactive split-slider or side-by-side view with crisp 8px rounded corners. Top tags highlight tile type (e.g., *Porcelain Stoneware 60×120*, *45° Mitred Edges*).
- **Service & Transparent Pricing Cards:** Clear breakdowns of linear and square meter rates without hidden fees. Backgrounds use light stone tinting `#FBFBF9` with high-contrast text metrics.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Small, 4px/8px padded badges used for "Clean Workspace Guaranteed", "10-Year Experience", "Laser Precision". Outlined in `#5B6C5D` at 20% opacity with `#5B6C5D` text.

### Input Fields
- **Form Controls:** Clean input fields with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#D8CAB8` border, and 8px border-radius. On focus, outline shifts to `#1E4756` without fuzzy glow rings. Clear helper text underneath for dimensions or bathroom size estimates.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Units:** 20px squares with 4px border-radius. Checked state fills with `#1E4756` displaying an off-white checkmark. Custom radio rings with matching color behavior for selecting service tiers (e.g., "Full Bathroom Turnkey" vs "Tile Installation Only").

### Cleanliness & Guarantee Callout Strip
- A dedicated layout component featuring soft olive/sand shading (`#F2F1ED`), emphasizing post-work dust removal, industrial vacuum cleanup, and personal warranty directly from the craftsman.
